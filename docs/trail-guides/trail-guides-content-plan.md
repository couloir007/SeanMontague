## Positioning

- No authoritative, structured guide to Kingdom Trails exists, and KT's own site is thin (April 2026 gap analysis).
- Advantages: Sean's firsthand mapping of the whole network, no ads or affiliates, structured pages.
- The biggest gap: no curated first-visit routing. Existing guides list trails; none gives a decision-free first day.
